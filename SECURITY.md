# Security

Please report a security problem in TITAN.ATLAS Free privately by email to **atlas@titanatlas.live**. Do not open a public issue.

Include:
- the installer name and SHA-256, or the version shown in the app;
- your operating system and version;
- what you found, and how to reproduce it.

Do not send passwords, tokens, mail content or other people's data. A description and the steps are enough.

We will acknowledge your report by email.
